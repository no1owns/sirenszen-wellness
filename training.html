Hands-On Education

Massage Therapy Training & CEUs

SirensZen is a certified teacher with a background in special education. Trained and mentored under apprenticeships — offering hands-on training and support for therapists in school or pursuing continuing education.

What We Offer

  • Hands-on training and mentorship for massage therapy students, shadowing real client sessions and building technique under direct supervision
  • Support for therapists pursuing CEUs (Continuing Education Units) needed to maintain licensure
  • Study and practice guidance to pass State and National Exams, including the MBLEx
  • Apprenticeship-based learning rooted in real clinical experience

Who It's For

A background in special education shapes how training is taught here — patient, hands-on, and adapted to how each student actually learns. Whether you're newly certified and building confidence with real clients, or a licensed therapist working through CEU requirements, training is tailored to where you are.
