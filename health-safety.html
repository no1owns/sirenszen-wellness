Health & Safety

We are committed to providing a clean, safe, and comfortable environment for every client. The following protocols are in place at every session.

  • Massage table sanitized before and after each client
  • Appointments scheduled with 1 hour cleaning time between sessions
  • Masks worn by therapist — optional for clients once on the table
  • Clients may remain face down (prone) for the majority or all of their session if preferred

Common Questions

Health & safety

What sanitation protocols do you follow?

The massage table is sanitized before and after every client.

Is there time between appointments for cleaning?

Yes. Appointments are scheduled with a full hour of cleaning time between sessions.

Do I need to wear a mask during my session?

The therapist wears a mask. It's optional for clients once they're on the table.

Can I stay face-down for my whole session?

Yes, if that's more comfortable for you. Clients may remain face down (prone) for the majority or all of their session.
