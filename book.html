Common Questions

Booking & appointments

How do I book a session?

Fill out the form above and choose your preferred duration, location (studio or mobile), and modality. SirensZen will follow up to confirm your appointment.

Is SirensZen appointment-only?

Yes. Sessions are by appointment only, available at our therapist's location or in-home.

Do you come to me, or do I come to you?

Both options are available. Choose an at-studio session or a mobile session where SirensZen comes to your home.

Can I talk to someone before booking a full session?

Yes, phone consultations are available for questions or wellness guidance. The first 15 minutes are free; planning and advising after that is $15.

What are SirensZen's hours?

Tuesday through Friday, 3PM–8PM. Saturday and Sunday by appointment. Closed Mondays.
